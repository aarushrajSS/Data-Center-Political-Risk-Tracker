How the scores work

State risk (1 to 5) is an analyst judgment from enacted policy, pending bills, local moratoriums, voter sentiment, and the likely Nov 3 winner's stated position. Site score (0 to 100) = state risk ÷ 5 × 100 × power factor (energized and running 0.45, partly energized 0.6, contracted but not live 0.75, none contracted 1.0). Override: a site with no power contracted or an undisclosed power source, in a state with heavy opposition (risk 3.5 or higher: TX, NY, MI, WI, GA, OH, NJ, NM), scores at least 80 and is always High. Bond score is the MW-weighted average of the sites behind it. Tiers: High 65+, Elevated 45 to 64, Moderate 30 to 44, Low under 30.

Rent: disclosed contract value ÷ term where available; otherwise $1.8M per MW-year. Oracle and Meta lease values are not disclosed per site and are all estimates; Oracle's 10-Q shows $288B of leases not yet commenced (FQ2'27 to FY29, 15-19 yr terms) and CoreWeave's shows $35.5B (2026-2029). Compute contracts (IREN/Microsoft, Nebius/Microsoft) are not leases and are excluded. Coordinates are approximate. Research tool, not investment advice.
